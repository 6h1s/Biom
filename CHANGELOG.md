# Biom versions

Numbering is MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH:
- **MAJOR**: a big redesign, or a change in how data is stored (may need a re-import).
- **MINOR**: new screens or features.
- **PATCH**: fixes and small visual tweaks.

## 1.2.2 (8 Nov 2026)
- Check for Updates in Settings › Software Update and in What's New.
- Reload Biom from Settings if a screen looks out of date.

## 1.2.1 (8 Nov 2026)
- Clearer Gym screen when no sets are in Biom yet, with a button to import your Watch gym log.

## 1.2.0 (8 Nov 2026)
- Biom checks for a new version each time you open it and offers a one-tap update, including from the Home Screen.

## 1.1.2 (8 Nov 2026)
- Version number on every screen. Tap it to see what's new.

## 1.1.1 (8 Nov 2026)
- Biom logo on the welcome screen and in About.
- New app icon and browser icon.

## 1.1.0 (8 Nov 2026)
- Overnight Vitals: sleep, heart and breathing against your typical ranges.
- Highlights that cross sleep, activity, heart and workouts.
- Training Load in Workouts.
- In Context on each workout: share of your Move ring and how you recovered.
- Explanations (ⓘ) on every section, and plain-language chart ranges.

## 1.0.0 (8 Nov 2026)
- First version: Summary, Workouts, Gym and Health.
- Import from Apple Health, gym log from the Watch, sync link and backups.
